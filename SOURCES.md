# Sources and live checks

The itinerary is a planning artifact, not a live booking feed. These are the sources used for route, attraction, access, lodging feature and operating-status claims. Verify availability and conditions again before booking and before driving.

## Official parks and access

1. Parks Canada, Banff planning: <https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/visit>
   - Banff visitor planning, fees, parking, wildlife and current visitor information.
2. Parks Canada, Lake Louise and Moraine Lake access: <https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/visit/parkbus/louise>
   - Shuttle reservations are required; Moraine Lake Road is closed to personal vehicles; 2026 shuttle season is listed as 15 May to 12 October for Lake Louise and 1 June to 12 October for Moraine Lake, weather permitting.
3. Parks Canada, Yoho planning: <https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/yoho/visit>
   - Highway 1 access, Field services, accommodation planning and park-pass context.
4. Parks Canada, Jasper planning: <https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper/visit>
   - Jasper visitor planning, Icefields Parkway, current status and closures links.
5. Parks Canada, park passes and fees: <https://parks.canada.ca/voyage-travel/admission>
   - Canada Strong Pass ended 7 Sep 2026; regular admission applies after that date.
6. Parks Canada, Jasper 2026 bulletin: <https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper/bulletins/76ECAE58-8A63-480C-8304-DC903837EEFD>
   - Edith Cavell Road and Maligne Canyon closure information used in the itinerary caveats.
